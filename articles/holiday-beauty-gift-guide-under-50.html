How to Choose the Right Beauty Gift

Start with what she already uses

A refill of a favorite product or a nearby shade, size or scent is usually safer than something entirely new. Notice which lip balm, perfume or moisturizer she reaches for.

Keep skin sensitivity in mind

If she has sensitive skin or fragrance sensitivities, choose fragrance-free options and read the ingredient list. If you are unsure, lip care, hand cream and basic moisturizer are lower-risk choices than strong skincare treatments.

Be careful with shades and scents

Complexion makeup and fragrance are the most personal categories. Choose them only if you know her preferences, and keep the receipt in case she wants to exchange it.

Think about presentation

A small bundle in a simple pouch or box feels more considered than a single product. Mixing a practical item with a small treat is an easy approach.

Common Gift Mistakes to Avoid

  • Buying strong actives such as high-strength exfoliants or retinoids for someone whose skin you do not know.
  • Choosing foundation or concealer without knowing her shade.
  • Ordering late, since shipping dates and stock can change during the holiday season.
  • Assuming the sale price will last. Check the price again before checkout.
